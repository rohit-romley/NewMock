# Mockaccino - Brand & Style Guidelines

## 1. Introduction & Brand Foundation

This document outlines the brand identity, visual style, and communication guidelines for the Mockaccino mobile application. Adhering to these guidelines ensures consistency and reinforces our brand across all user touchpoints.

* **Mission Statement:** To empower professionals to confidently advance their careers by providing an accessible platform for mock interviews and valuable referral exchanges.
* **Vision Statement:** To be the leading mobile platform for peer-to-peer career preparation and networking, fostering a global community of supportive professionals.
* **Brand Values:**
    * **Collaboration:** Connecting professionals to help each other succeed.
    * **Growth:** Facilitating skill development and career advancement.
    * **Trust:** Building a secure and reliable environment for genuine interaction.
    * **Accessibility:** Making career support readily available to everyone.
    * **Professionalism:** Maintaining a high standard of interaction and presentation.
* **Brand Personality:**
    * Supportive & Encouraging
    * Credible & Trustworthy
    * Modern & Streamlined
    * Efficient & Approachable
* **Target Audience Summary:** Active job seekers, career changers, recent graduates, and professionals seeking interview practice or networking opportunities for job referrals.

## 2. Logo Usage

* **Logo Concept Description:** The Mockaccino logo concept combines elements representing professionalism (a tie) and connection/conversation (a coffee cup, often associated with networking or informal chats)[cite: 2]. The steam adds a touch of dynamism. It aims for a clever, memorable, and modern mark relevant to the professional networking context.
* **Primary Logo Description:** The full logo includes the stylized coffee cup/tie icon along with the "Mockaccino" wordmark, typically presented as shown in the initial branding mockups[cite: 2].
* **Logo Variations:**
    * **Icon Only:** The coffee cup/tie icon can be used alone where space is limited (e.g., favicons, small UI elements), provided the brand is already established.
    * **Wordmark Only:** The "Mockaccino" wordmark can be used in specific contexts like headers if needed[cite: 9].
    * **Monochromatic:** Simplified white or black versions for use on colored or photographic backgrounds.
* **Clear Space:** Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equivalent to the height of the tie element within the icon. No other elements (text, graphics) should intrude into this space.
* **Minimum Size:** The full logo should not be reproduced smaller than 60 pixels wide in digital applications. The icon-only version should not be smaller than 24x24 pixels.
* **Incorrect Usage:**
    * Do not stretch, condense, or alter the logo's proportions.
    * Do not change the logo's colors outside the defined palette variations.
    * Do not rotate the logo.
    * Do not place the logo on visually cluttered or low-contrast backgrounds that impair readability.
    * Do not add outlines, shadows, or other visual effects.

## 4. Typography

Typography should be clean, legible, and modern, reflecting the app's professional and efficient nature. A sans-serif typeface is required.

* **Primary Typeface:** Inter (Available on Google Fonts) - Chosen for its excellent readability on screens, range of weights, and modern aesthetic.
* **Font Weights & Styles:**
    * **Headings (H1, H2, Screen Titles):** Semibold (600)
    * **Subheadings & Major Labels:** Medium (500)
    * **Body Text (Profiles, Chat):** Regular (400) [cite: 11, 34]
    * **Buttons & Captions:** Medium (500) or Semibold (600) [cite: 6, 30]
    * **Emphasis:** Use Medium or Semibold inline where needed, sparingly. Avoid italics unless necessary for specific conventions.
* **Hierarchy & Sizing (Example Mobile Scale - use sp/pt):**
    * **H1 / Screen Title:** 24pt, Semibold, Line Height: 32pt (e.g., "Sign-up" [cite: 4])
    * **H2 / Section Title:** 20pt, Semibold, Line Height: 28pt (e.g., "Select your Current Role" [cite: 6])
    * **Body Large / Profile Names:** 18pt, Medium, Line Height: 24pt (e.g., "Edward Kenway" [cite: 9])
    * **Body Default / Core Text:** 16pt, Regular, Line Height: 24pt (e.g., Profile Bio [cite: 11])
    * **Button Text:** 16pt, Medium, Line Height: 20pt (e.g., "Continue" [cite: 6])
    * **Captions / Labels:** 14pt, Regular/Medium, Line Height: 20pt (e.g., Field labels like "Email" [cite: 4])
    * **Small Text / Timestamps:** 12pt, Regular, Line Height: 16pt (e.g., Chat timestamp [cite: 18])
* **Usage:**
    * Default to left-alignment for body copy and lists for optimal readability. Center-alignment can be used for short headlines or specific UI elements like buttons.
    * Ensure adequate line height (approx. 1.4x - 1.6x font size) for readability.
    * Maintain consistent type scale and hierarchy across the application.

## 5. Imagery & Photography

Imagery should feel authentic, professional, and supportive.

* **Style Guidelines:** Authentic, diverse professionals in realistic work or collaboration settings. Focus on connection, focused individuals (can be stock photos, but prioritize diversity and natural poses), and clean, modern workspace environments. Avoid overly staged, generic corporate stock photos. Aim for a bright, optimistic, and encouraging feel. Illustrations should be clean and simple[cite: 3, 4, 8].
* **Color Treatment:** Natural colors, well-lit environments. Avoid heavy filters or overly stylized treatments.
* **Subject Matter:** Appropriate themes include:
    * Networking (subtle, professional context).
    * People collaborating or in discussion.
    * Individuals focused on work (e.g., using laptops, reviewing notes).
    * Clean, professional headshots for user profile examples[cite: 9, 10].
    * Abstract or simple illustrations representing concepts like connection, growth, or communication[cite: 8, 10].

## 6. Iconography

Icons should be clear, consistent, and intuitive.

* **Icon Style:** Use a consistent library (e.g., Material Symbols, Feather Icons, or a custom set). Recommended style: Clean, filled or bold outline style with consistent stroke weight. Icons should be easily recognizable at small sizes (e.g., 20x20 or 24x24 pixels). Examples include navigation icons[cite: 17], settings gear[cite: 9], back arrow[cite: 6], checkmarks[cite: 6], like/heart[cite: 8], chat bubbles, profile icons[cite: 5], etc.
* **Usage Guidelines:** Use icons to aid navigation, represent features visually, clarify actions on buttons, and enhance scannability. Ensure icons are clearly associated with their corresponding function or label. Use tooltips or labels for less universally understood icons if necessary.

## 7. Layout & Composition

The layout should prioritize clarity, ease of use, and a mobile-first experience.

* **Principles:** Clean, uncluttered interfaces. Intuitive navigation patterns standard for iOS and Android. Ample white space to reduce cognitive load. Consistency in spacing, alignment, and component placement across all screens.
* **Grid/Spacing:** Utilize a standard spacing unit, preferably based on an 8dp grid system (e.g., margins and paddings of 8dp, 16dp, 24dp, etc.). This ensures visual harmony and rhythm throughout the application. Consistent vertical and horizontal alignment is key.

## 8. Brand Voice & Tone

Our voice is professional yet approachable, aiming to build trust and encourage users.

* **Brand Voice:**
    * **Professional:** Respectful, knowledgeable, and focused on career-related interactions.
    * **Encouraging:** Positive, supportive, and motivating.
    * **Clear:** Easy to understand, avoiding jargon or ambiguity.
    * **Concise:** Gets straight to the point, respecting users' time.
    * **Supportive:** Empathetic and helpful, especially in guidance and error states.
* **Tone Variations:** Adapt the tone based on the context:
    * *Onboarding/Instructional:* Helpful, simple, direct (e.g., "Select your Current Role"[cite: 6], "Enter your Invite Code" [cite: 2]).
    * *Success Messages/Matches:* Positive, celebratory, brief (e.g., "It's a Match!"[cite: 30], "You matched with [Name]." [cite: 30]).
    * *Error Messages/Limits:* Empathetic, clear, providing solutions or next steps (e.g., "You're out of Likes. Get more likes in [Time] or Get Unlimited Likes with Premium." [cite: 42]).
    * *Premium Upsell:* Value-focused, benefit-driven, non-intrusive (e.g., "+ Unlimited Swiping", "+ See who Matched with you"[cite: 41], "Try Premium" [cite: 13, 24]).
    * *Chat Interface:* Neutral, allowing user personalities to dominate, but system messages should be clear and helpful[cite: 34].
* **Vocabulary:**
    * Use professional but accessible language. Avoid overly technical jargon or overly casual slang.
    * Use terms like "connection," "match," "professional," "user." [cite: 30]
    * Refer to core features consistently (e.g., "Mock Interview," "Referral Exchange" [cite: 8, 11]).

## 9. Editorial Style Guide

Consistency in writing style reinforces brand professionalism.

* **Capitalization:**
    * Use **Sentence case** for most button labels (e.g., "Sign up"[cite: 4], "Log in"[cite: 5], "Send a message" [cite: 28]), list items, and body text.
    * Use **Title Case** for major screen titles (e.g., "Select your Current Role"[cite: 6], "Edit Profile" [cite: 38]), and proper nouns (e.g., "LinkedIn," "Apple" [cite: 3]).
    * Follow standard capitalization for user input fields (e.g., "Full Name," "Password" [cite: 4]).
* **Punctuation:**
    * Use the Oxford comma (serial comma) in lists of three or more items for clarity.
    * Use periods at the end of complete sentences in descriptions, instructions, and error messages. Avoid periods in short labels or titles where possible unless it's a full sentence.
* **Abbreviations/Acronyms:** Spell out acronyms on first use if they aren't widely understood by the target audience (e.g., UI/UX is acceptable, but a less common one should be spelled out first).
* **Naming:**
    * The official application name is **Mockaccino**. Use this consistently.
